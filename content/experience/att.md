I spent five years at AT&T in customer, campaign, and pricing analytics, and I'm a graduate of its Finance Leadership Program.

## Lead Financial Analyst

2022–2024
{.role-line}

- Partnered with marketing and operations leaders on a predictive retention dashboard that surfaces early churn signals, used to steer campaign strategy across markets and segments.
- Analyzed campaign performance and cross-sell trends to isolate engagement drivers and the best product bundle mixes. Built the measurement plan behind a targeted 5% year-over-year churn reduction and a 15% annual lift in cross-sell conversion.
- Automated customer analytics workflows in Python during an enterprise analytics modernization, speeding up segmentation reporting and time-to-decision.

## Financial Analyst

2019–2022
{.role-line}

- Designed and scaled the centralized dashboard tracking customer lifetime value, churn, and ARPU, used by senior marketing leadership for quarterly investment decisions.
- Designed dynamic pricing models evaluating product profitability and value drivers through the Finance Leadership Program.
- Translated analytics into leadership-ready narratives on product performance, buyer behavior, and campaign ROI, presented to executives monthly.
