Level Blue is a cybersecurity and managed security services company. I work with the product management and development organization on retention, revenue, and customer analytics.

## What I've done

- **Retention and revenue modeling.** Developed scenario-based cohort models of partner and customer retention, churn, revenue growth, and cross-sell performance, projecting a $2.5M annual revenue uplift.
- **Unified customer data.** Directed a customer data enrichment program that integrated disparate source systems into a single analytics warehouse. Forecast accuracy improved about 20%, and the unified data made segmentation modeling possible.
- **Automated pipelines.** Engineered Python ETL pipelines that deliver real-time visibility into customer transactions and behavior signals, eliminating more than 1,000 hours of manual reporting a year and $100K in projected third-party data costs.
