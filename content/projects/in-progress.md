These projects are under way. Each repository goes public when the project ships.

## Retention Uplift Engine

Causal uplift modeling on 13.9 million randomized ad-exposure records. The project compares targeting policies using Qini evaluation and includes a Streamlit simulator for testing them.

## Ticket Price Intelligence

An automated daily pipeline that tracks NBA and NFL secondary-market ticket prices. It covers price-decay modeling and opponent effects, and feeds an executive dashboard in Tableau.

## Marketing Measurement Lab

Bayesian marketing mix modeling with Google Meridian, paired with synthetic-control geo-lift analysis. Both are validated against simulated data where the true effect is known.
