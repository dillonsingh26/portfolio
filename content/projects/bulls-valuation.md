A five-year valuation and trade-impact forecasting model for the Chicago Bulls, built during my M.S. in Data Science at Northwestern.

## What I built

- A valuation model that integrates brand asset worth, player statistics, salary, and sales data
- A trade-impact forecast showing how roster moves change the five-year outlook
- A benchmark against comparable NBA franchises

## Player value per dollar

One part of the analysis compares each player's production per $1M of salary against the league at their position, using three measures: Player Efficiency Rating (PER), Win Shares (WS), and Value Over Replacement Player (VORP).
